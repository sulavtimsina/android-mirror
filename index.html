Mirror your Android phone

  1. On the phone, turn on Developer options → USB debugging.
  2. Connect it to this computer with a USB cable.
  3. If you have adb installed, run adb kill-server first. Only one program can use the phone at a time.
  4. Press Connect phone, pick your phone in the browser's list, then tap Allow on the phone.

Runs entirely in your browser over WebUSB. Nothing is uploaded. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Click/drag = touch · Right click = back · Middle click = home · Wheel = scroll · Click the screen, then type · Ctrl/⌘+V pastes your clipboard · Uses scrcpy and Tango
